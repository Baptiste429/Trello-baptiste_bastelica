The Story Behind Bakeology

Nestled at 43 Meath Street with its warm yellow facade[cite: 3], Bakeology Treats brings the authentic comfort of Argentine bakeries straight to The Liberties, Dublin.

Every morning our display fills up with handmade empanadas, warm medialunas, savoury ciabattas, and classic cornstarch alfajores with dulce de leche[cite: 2, 4].
